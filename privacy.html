Privacy Policy — Liar's Dice: Bluff Offline

Last updated: 30 September 2026

This policy explains what information the game "Liar's Dice: Bluff Offline" (the "app"), published by TE Software ("we"), handles and why. The short version: the game runs on your phone, we have no servers and no user accounts, and we do not collect personal data ourselves. Ads are served by Google AdMob and purchases go through Google Play; those Google services collect some data as described below. The web version, Liar's Dice Tavern, is covered in section 9.

1. Data that stays on your device

The app saves the following on your phone so that the game remembers where you left off:

  • your settings (language, number of players, rule variants, difficulty, sound);
  • career progress, dice statistics, whether you finished the tutorial, and which table, dice and cup you picked;
  • the IDs of items you bought (for example remove_ads) and a match counter used to space out ads;
  • a short technical log of the current and the previous session (warnings and errors), used to find crashes.

This data is not sent to us or anyone else. The log leaves your phone only if you choose to send it: tapping the version label five times copies it to the clipboard, and you decide where to paste it. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes all of it. If backup is turned on for your device, Android's own backup may include these files; that backup is handled by Google under your Google account settings.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads through the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob): at most one full-screen ad between matches, every 3 matches, never during play, plus an optional ad you can choose to watch to try a locked theme for one match. When your phone is online and ads are requested, the SDK collects and shares with Google:

  • the advertising ID and app set ID of your device;
  • your IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location;
  • ad interactions (for example ad views and taps) and diagnostic information about app and SDK performance.

Google uses this data to serve and measure ads, for analytics and to prevent fraud. How Google uses data from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences.

Your choices. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown; you can change your choice at any time under Settings → Privacy → Privacy options. Everywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on the device). The optional purchase "Remove ads" turns off the ads between matches.

If your phone is offline, no ads are requested and the game works fully.

3. Purchases (Google Play)

In-app purchases are handled by Google Play Billing. Your payment details go to Google, never to us. The app only learns which items you own and stores their IDs on your device (section 1); we run no purchase server and keep no purchase records. Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

4. What we do not do

  • No analytics or tracking tools of our own, and no crash-reporting service.
  • No account, sign-in, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise location.
  • No selling of data. No online multiplayer or chat: pass and play is played on one phone.

5. Children

The app is meant for users aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app itself collects no personal information (section 1); if you believe a child has used the app, you can clear its data by uninstalling it, and reset the device's advertising ID as described in section 2.

6. Security and retention

Data in section 1 stays on your device until you delete the app or its data. Data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit and kept by Google under Google's policies.

7. Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. For data collected by Google for ads, use the controls in section 2 or Google's own tools. You can contact us with any privacy question.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top. Material changes will also be noted in the app's store listing.

9. Web version (Liar's Dice Tavern)

"Liar's Dice Tavern" is the same game made to run in a web browser, published on CrazyGames (crazygames.com). In the web version:

  • there are no ads and no purchases: the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google Play Billing are not part of it, so sections 2 and 3 do not apply;
  • your settings, career progress, dice statistics and whether you finished the tutorial are kept in your browser's storage for the game's site (IndexedDB) on your device and are not sent to us; clearing that site's data in your browser deletes them;
  • no session log is kept, and there is no account or sign-in;
  • the only link, "Privacy policy" in Settings, opens this page in a new tab.

The web page around the game is operated by CrazyGames, which may use cookies, analytics and ads under its own privacy policy: https://www.crazygames.com/privacy-policy. CrazyGames shows us only aggregated statistics about the game (such as the number of plays and the average play time), not data about individual players.
